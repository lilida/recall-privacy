RECALL Brain Games – Support

Need help with the app? Email us at lilida@lilida.org and we will reply as soon as we can.

Frequently asked questions

How do I use the app without an account?

On the first screen, tap Continue as Guest. Guest results are not saved to an account.

How do I delete my account and data?

Signed-in users can go to Home → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and all game results. If Delete Account asks you to sign in again, sign out, sign back in, and retry. You can also email us to request deletion.

Voice analysis is slow or fails.

Voice analysis needs an internet connection and microphone permission (Settings → RECALL → Microphone). If the service has been idle, the first request can take up to a minute. Please try again.

Sign in with Apple or Google isn’t working.

Check your internet connection, update to the latest version of the app, and try again. If it still fails, email us with a short description of the error.

Is this a medical app?

No. RECALL Brain Games is for general wellness and informational purposes only. It is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice.
