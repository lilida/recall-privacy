RECALL Brain Games – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

This policy explains what information the RECALL Brain Games iOS app (“the app”) collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your name, email address, and a unique user ID from the sign-in provider (Apple may provide a private relay email). If you use the app as a guest, no account is created and no account information is collected.
  • Game results. For signed-in users, scores, timings, accuracy and related performance data from the games, along with your user ID and the time of play.
  • Voice recordings. If you use the voice analysis feature, the app records audio from your microphone, only after you grant permission and start a recording.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers, and we do not use the camera.

How we use information

  • To provide the app’s features: signing you in, saving and showing your game results, and analyzing your speech.
  • To keep the service working and secure.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Voice analysis and third-party services

When you submit a recording, it is sent to our server, which forwards it to OpenAI to produce a transcript and then to analyze that transcript for speech patterns such as pauses and filler words. Our server deletes the audio file after processing and does not store the recording. OpenAI processes the data under its own terms and privacy policy (openai.com/policies/privacy-policy).

Account and game data are stored using Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Sign-in is provided by Apple and Google, whose privacy policies apply to their sign-in services.

Data retention and deletion

Your account and game results are kept until you delete your account. You can delete your account and all associated game results at any time in the app: Home → Delete Account. You can also email us at the address below to request deletion.

Not a medical device

The app is provided for general wellness and informational purposes only. It is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows when it last changed.
